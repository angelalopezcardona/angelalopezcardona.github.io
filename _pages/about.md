  I am a **researcher** specializing in Large Language Models, multimodal architectures, and cognitive-signal integration for human alignment and interpretability. My work includes reward modeling (RLHF, DPO), dataset creation, and cognitively inspired training pipelines developed during my industrial PhD at [Telefónica Innovación Digital](https://telefonicainnovaciondigital.com) and [Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC)](https://www.upc.edu/es) under the supervision of [Sergi Abadal](https://sergiabadal.com) and [Ioannis Arapakis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ioannisarapakis/). 
  
   Before transitioning into research, I spent four years leading end-to-end development of **AI-driven products** in the energy sector. I am currently in my third year of the PhD and hold two Master’s degrees: one in [Industrial Engineering](https://muei.etseib.masters.upc.edu/en) and another in [Master's in Artificial Intelligence](https://masters.fib.upc.edu/masters/master-artificial-intelligence).
